Decision Confidence for Product Audits

Qutis AI Research — product rules-engine scoring (v1)

This note defines how we combine intake context, personalization mode, ingredient evidence, and vision-derived audit confidence into a single decision confidence score Dc​∈[0,1], and how Dc​ drives user-facing guidance tone. It complements the confidence estimation framework for skin audits, which derives vision confidence from token log-probabilities. Here the focus is cosmetic product review after label read and rules enrichment.

1. Building User Context

Before a skin snapshot is registered, the agent persists structured profile facts through intake (goals, allergies, current products, skin type, pregnancy, breastfeeding, and related notes). An optional skin image review adds a structured audit (concern, condition signals, derived needs). Together, intake and audit supply the inputs that later become the context score C; they are normalized on [0,1] when the rules engine runs.

Three scalars

Symbol Meaning
V Vision confidence — geometric mean of token probabilities on the model’s observation span (skin-audit paper).
K Audit completeness — share of structured skin-audit checks that are filled (concern, severity, signals, derived needs).
h Profile richness — whether the product request carries stated user context (profile fields, product name, notes, etc.).

Stated profile feeds h; the photo path feeds V and K. §2 selects personalization mode; §4 defines how h, V, and K combine into C for each mode.

2. Personalization Mode and Confidence Ceilings

Mode m fixes how strongly a product audit may use skin and profile context, and which terms enter decision confidence later. It is chosen from intake outcome and user preference, not from Dc​ itself.

2.1 Choosing m

Priority order (first match wins):

  1. generic — user explicitly opts out of a skin snapshot (skin_snapshot_skipped).
  2. personalized — fresh skin audit with usable observations (skin snapshot + product review path).
  3. profile_only — no fresh audit, but stated profile exists (goals, allergies, products, skin type, pregnancy, etc.) and the user checks a product.
  4. generic — otherwise (no audit, insufficient profile).
Mode m Typical user journey
personalized Skin analysis and product review.
profile_only Product review only, with profile on file.
generic Product review only, minimal or no profile, or explicit skip of skin photo.

2.2 Mode ceiling πm​

Even with perfect ingredient evidence, reported decision confidence is capped by mode so copy cannot sound fully personalized when the user did not take the full skin-audit path:

Mode m Ceiling πm​
personalized 1.0
profile_only 0.65
generic 0.50

After the raw blend Draw​ is computed (§5),

Dc​=min(Draw​, πm​).

2.3 What each mode uses in the blend (preview)

Mode Vision term V in Draw​? Typical role of C
personalized Yes (25% weight in v1) Built from V and K (§4)
profile_only No separate V term Built from h (§4)
generic No Fixed neutral prior (§4)

§3 defines evidence coverage E; §4 defines C; §5 gives the full Draw​ equation.

3. Evidence Coverage E

After the INCI list is extracted from the label (or user input), the rules engine looks up each line in the CIR ingredient index. Evidence coverage measures how much of that list is backed by retrievable safety evidence—not how severe any rule hit is, only whether we can cite indexed CIR data for each ingredient.

Let N be the number of ingredients on the request and, for each position i=1,…,N, let indexed(i) be true when that line resolves to an indexed ingredient with a stable id. Then

E=⎩⎨⎧​0N1​i=1∑N​indexed(i)​N=0N≥1​

with E clamped to [0,1]. Example: three INCI lines and two indexed → E=2/3.

E is the evidence term in decision confidence. In v1 it carries 40% weight when m=personalized and 55% when m∈{profile_only,generic} (§5).

4. Context Score C

Context score C∈[0,1] summarizes how much user and audit context is available for the active mode m. It uses the scalars from §1 (V, K, h) but is not the same as any one of them: C is the single number that enters the decision-confidence blend (§5).

4.1 Profile richness h (for profile_only)

On the product request, set h=1 if any of the following is non-empty: concerns, symptoms, skin type, age range, notes, escalation notes, or product name; otherwise h=0.

4.2 Audit completeness K (for personalized)

K is the fraction of four structured audit checks that pass: primary concern, condition severity, condition signals (inflammation, barrier, or patterns), and at least one derived need. With four checks,

K∈{0, 41​, 21​, 43​, 1}.

generic Fixed neutral prior (0.5); no skin audit in the blend.

In personalized mode, V also appears as its own term in Draw​ (25% in v1), so model certainty affects decision confidence both through C and through V directly (§5).

5. Decision Confidence Dc​

Decision confidence combines ingredient evidence E (§3), context score C (§4), and—only in personalized mode—vision confidence V (§1). Weights depend on mode m (§2). The result is capped by πm​, then mapped to guidance tone for product copy.

5.1 Raw blend Draw​

For personalized mode, V is taken from the skin audit when vision context is active; otherwise the vision term is zero.

Draw​=⎩⎨⎧​0.40E+0.35C+0.25V0.55E+0.45C​m=personalizedm∈{profile_only, generic}​

All inputs lie in [0,1], so Draw​∈[0,1].

Expanded personalized form. When C=2V+K​ (the usual case with audit data), substituting gives

Draw​=0.40E+0.425V+0.175K,

so the declared 25% vision weight plus the share of V inside 35% context yields 42.5% total influence from V on Draw​.

5.2 Final score Dc​

Apply the mode ceiling from §2.2:

Dc​=min(Draw​, πm​),Dc​∈[0,1].

Dc​ is what we log as decision_confidence and use for routing tone below.

5.3 Tier, guidance mode, and tone

Dc​ does not change rules-engine safety findings; it controls how assertive the user-facing product report may be.

Dc​ Confidence tier Guidance mode Tone (product report)
Dc​≥0.75 high full Clear safety themes; concise recommendation when evidence supports it.
0.50≤Dc​<0.75 moderate cautious Hedged language; note evidence gaps; avoid definitive fit verdicts.
Dc​<0.50 low clarify Short report; stress uncertainty; ask to confirm product or send a clearer label photo.

Personalization mode (§2) still constrains what the report may say (vision vs profile vs generic disclaimers). Guidance mode constrains how confidently it may say it.

6. Personalized mode: summing the three scalars

In personalized mode, decision confidence is a weighted sum of three scalars on [0,1]. Weights sum to 1: 40% evidence, 35% context, 25% vision.

6.1 Evidence term (40%)

Let N be the number of INCI lines on the product request. For each line i=1,…,N, let 1[indexed(i)] be 1 when that ingredient resolves in the CIR index with a stable id, and 0 otherwise (§3). Evidence coverage is

E=N1​i=1∑N​1[indexed(i)].

The evidence contribution to the blend is

0.40⋅E=0.40⋅N1​i=1∑N​1[indexed(i)].

6.2 Vision term (25%)

For the skin observation span, let p1​,…,pm​ be the model’s chosen-token probabilities on that span (from log-probabilities ℓk​ via pk​=eℓk​; see skin-audit paper). Vision confidence is the geometric mean

V=(k=1∏m​pk​)1/m.

The vision contribution to the blend is

0.25⋅V=0.25⋅(k=1∏m​pk​)1/m.

6.3 Context term (35%)

Context score C is defined in §4 (personalized: typically C=min(1, 2V+K​) when audit data exists). The context contribution is 0.35⋅C.

6.4 Combined sum

Draw​=0.40⋅N1​i=1∑N​1[indexed(i)]+0.35C+0.25⋅(k=1∏m​pk​)1/m.

Equivalently, using E and V as shorthand:

Draw​=0.40E+0.35C+0.25V.

For m=personalized, πm​=1.0, so

Dc​=min(Draw​, 1.0).

6.5 Fully expanded (when C=2V+K​)

Substituting C=21​(∏k=1m​pk​)1/m+2K​ (and clamping where the implementation applies):

Draw​=​0.40⋅N1​i=1∑N​1[indexed(i)]+0.175K+0.425⋅(k=1∏m​pk​)1/m.​

Here 0.425 on the geometric mean is 0.25 (direct vision weight) plus 0.35 × ½ (vision’s share inside context).

6.6 Worked example

Three INCI lines, all indexed (N=3, each indicator 1): E=1. Observation span with geometric mean V=0.80. Audit completeness K=1 → C=0.90.

Draw​​=0.40⋅31​(1+1+1)+0.35(0.90)+0.25(0.80)=0.40+0.315+0.20=0.915.​

Same value from the expanded form: 0.40(1)+0.175(1)+0.425(0.80)=0.915. Then Dc​=0.915 → tier high, guidance full (§5.3).
